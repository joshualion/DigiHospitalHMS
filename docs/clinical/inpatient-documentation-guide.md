# Inpatient Documentation Guide

Use the ward chart only for patients with an active admission.

## Ward Worklist

Open **Ward Charts** from the admin navigation. Active admitted or transferred patients appear with admission number, ward, bed, allergies and alerts. Open or create the chart from the worklist.

## Patient Chart

Record:

- Clinician progress notes using SOAP or another configured note type.
- Nursing notes by shift.
- Observation chart entries for temperature, pulse, respiration, blood pressure, oxygen saturation, pain, glucose and consciousness notes.
- Intake and output with the unit selected by staff.
- Nursing care plans with problem, goal, intervention, evaluation and status.
- Inpatient diagnoses/problem-list entries.
- Clinician orders for nursing care, monitoring, diet, activity and investigations.
- Shift handovers and acknowledgement.

Signed progress notes are immutable. Add an amendment with a reason when a signed note requires correction.

## Orders

Orders move through draft, active, acknowledged, completed, discontinued or cancelled. Staff worklists show active and acknowledged orders only.

## Discharge Summary

Draft the discharge summary from the chart, review it, edit the text as needed, then sign it. Signing the summary closes the inpatient chart but does not replace the admissions discharge workflow.

Medication administration, theatre, blood bank, insurance and automated decision support are not part of Phase 6B.
