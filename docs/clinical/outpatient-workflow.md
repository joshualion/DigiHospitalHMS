# Outpatient Workflow

Phase 2C starts from the Phase 2B queue.

1. Reception checks in an appointment or walk-in.
2. The patient appears on the clinical worklist at `/admin/clinical/worklist`.
3. An authorized clinician starts the encounter.
4. Nursing or authorized staff record vitals.
5. The clinician records presenting complaint, history, examination findings, diagnosis, plan, follow-up and optional referral recommendation.
6. The clinician signs the encounter.
7. The visit is completed, the queue entry is removed and any linked appointment is completed.

Allergies and important alerts are displayed prominently inside the encounter. The patient timeline includes encounters, vitals, allergies and alerts.

Signed records are not edited directly. Use an amendment with a reason to append a correction or clarification.

Do not enter prescriptions, orders, lab requests, radiology requests, pharmacy actions, billing, admissions or fabricated diagnostic guidance in this phase.
